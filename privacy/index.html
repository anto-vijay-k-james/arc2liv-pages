Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Arc2Liv is a local-first planning app. Goals, tasks, reminders, expenses, categories, and preferences are stored on your device unless you choose to export them or use Google Drive backup.

Information handled by Arc2Liv

If you connect a Google account, Arc2Liv receives basic account information such as your name, email address, profile identifier, and profile image. Authentication credentials are stored in secure device storage. Arc2Liv requests access to files it creates in Google Drive so it can create, list, and restore your backups.

The app may use device notification permissions to deliver reminders you create. Reminder information remains on your device.

How information is used

Information is used to provide planning, reminders, sign-in, backup, restore, import, and export features. Arc2Liv does not sell personal information and does not include advertising or cross-app tracking.

Sharing and service providers

When you use Google sign-in or Google Drive backup, information is exchanged with Google under Google’s terms and privacy policy. Files you export are shared only with destinations you select through your device’s sharing interface.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains on your device until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall the app. You can disconnect your Google account in Arc2Liv. You can delete Arc2Liv backup files from Google Drive. Google may retain information according to your Google account settings and policies.

For help deleting app information, follow the instructions on the support page.

Children

Arc2Liv is a general productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

Arc2Liv uses platform secure storage for authentication credentials. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Arc2Liv changes. The effective date will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the Arc2Liv support page.
