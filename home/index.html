A PLAN BUILT AROUND YOU

Create a Custom Goal
with AI using GPT.

Have a goal that needs a personal plan? Open the Arc2Liv Goal Builder GPT, describe what you want to achieve, and turn the result into a goal you can follow in the app.

  1. Open the Goal Builder GPT. In Arc2Liv, choose AI Goal Builder, then Create a Custom Goal. Tap Open Goal Builder GPT to continue in ChatGPT.
  2. Describe your goal. Share your target, timeframe, and preferences. Answer the GPT’s questions to shape a plan with scheduled tasks, action steps, reminders, and budgets.
  3. Download your plan. Save the generated JSON file when your custom plan is ready.
  4. Import it into Arc2Liv. Return to Create Custom Goal and choose Import or Paste JSON. Import the downloaded file or paste its contents to bring your plan into the app.

You choose what to share with the external AI assistant. Review the generated plan and adjust it to suit your day.

Goals overview

See your ongoing goals and their progress at a glance. Switch between ongoing, completed, and all goals, or search for the plan you want to work on.

Add a goal

Give your goal a title, add an optional description and planned budget, choose a currency, and set the target date required to save your goal.

Use Add Goal to build a plan manually, or choose AI Goal Builder to start with a ready plan or a custom AI-assisted goal.

Tasks

Turn your plan into daily action.

Organize the work behind your goals with scheduled tasks, reminders, and smaller action steps.

Tasks overview

Choose a date from the weekly calendar to review your work. Search or filter tasks, switch between upcoming and completed items, and mark finished tasks complete.

Task details

See the task objective, date, time, priority, and difficulty together. Open its linked goal or reminder, review related expenses, and follow the numbered action steps.

Add a task

Write a task title and optional objective, add action steps, and link the task to a goal. Choose a planned date and time, set a reminder, and select a daily, weekly, or monthly repeat.

Expenses

Understand what your goals cost.

Keep payment details connected to the goals and tasks they support.

Expense tools

Review expenses for the selected date and switch between pending, paid, and all payments. Use the expense menu to record a new expense or organize categories and subcategories.

Add an expense

Record the title, amount, date, and optional notes. Choose a category and subcategory, set the payment status, and link the expense to a goal or task.

Expense details

Review the amount, payment status, priority, category, and connected goal or task. When a payment is complete, swipe the status handle to mark it paid.

Insights

See how your plan is progressing.

Review your goal momentum and spending together so you can decide what needs attention.

Goal and expense insights

Select a goal to see overall completion, completed tasks, and days remaining. The status bar separates pending, in-progress, and completed work. The expense overview shows spending so far, the planned budget, and pending payments, with expense breakdowns below.
