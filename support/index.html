Support

Get help with Arc2Liv, report a problem, or suggest an improvement.

Before reporting a problem

  • Update Arc2Liv to the latest available version.
  • Restart the app and your device.
  • For backup issues, confirm that Google Drive access is connected.
  • Include your device model, Android version, app version, and the steps that reproduce the issue.

Contact support

Open an Arc2Liv support request. Do not include passwords, access tokens, financial account details, backup files, or other sensitive personal information.

Delete your data

  • Delete individual goals, tasks, reminders, and expenses inside Arc2Liv.
  • Use the app’s reset option to remove all locally stored Arc2Liv data.
  • Delete Arc2Liv backup files from your Google Drive account.
  • Disconnect Arc2Liv from your Google account in the app and, if desired, revoke its access from your Google Account permissions.

Uninstalling Arc2Liv removes its local app data from the device but does not delete backup files you placed in Google Drive.
